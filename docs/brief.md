# Resumr

> ✨ Modern and simple open-source resume generator.

---

# 📄 Product Summary

**Resumr** is an open-source web application focused on helping users create clean, professional resumes quickly and with minimal friction.

The product is designed around simplicity, speed, and clarity instead of overwhelming users with complex builders, unnecessary customization, or distracting interfaces.

Resumr lives under the personal/open-source ecosystem of `salazarjs.dev` as:

`resumr.salazarjs.dev`

The branding follows a modern developer-oriented identity, similar to other minimalist tech products and tools.

---

# 🎯 Product Goal

The primary goal of Resumr is:

> Help users generate professional resumes quickly, clearly, and without friction.

The product prioritizes:
- ⚡ Speed
- 🧠 Simplicity
- 📄 Clarity
- ✨ Clean design
- 🪶 Lightweight experience
- 🌍 Accessibility
- 🔓 Open-source transparency

---

# ❗ Problem We Are Solving

Creating resumes today is often:
- Slow
- Overcomplicated
- Filled with unnecessary forms
- Locked behind paywalls
- Overloaded with templates and features
- Difficult to edit or export cleanly

Many resume builders focus more on monetization than usability.

Users frequently experience:
- Friction during creation
- Confusing interfaces
- Forced account creation
- Watermarks or paid exports
- Poor formatting
- Lack of ownership over their data

---

# 💡 Resumr Solution

Resumr solves this by offering:

- ✍️ Fast resume creation
- 🎯 Minimal and focused UX
- 📄 Professional layouts
- ⚡ Instant editing experience
- 🔓 Open-source transparency
- 🧠 Intelligent assistance without complexity
- 💾 User ownership and portability
- 🪶 Lightweight and responsive interface

---

# 🧭 Product Philosophy

## 1. Simplicity First
The interface should feel obvious and clean.

## 2. Frictionless Experience
Creating or editing a resume should take seconds.

## 3. Clarity Over Features
The focus is the resume itself, not endless customization.

## 4. Fast by Default
Speed is part of the product experience.

## 5. Open and Accessible
Users should feel ownership and transparency over their resumes and data.
